About Stock Pro

Ice Cream Stock Pro is a fully independent, offline-first Progressive Web Application (PWA) tailored specifically for ice cream vendors, distributors, and shopkeepers to manage cups, sticks, spoons, and flavors effortlessly.

Security & Privacy

Zero Cloud Tracking: This application does not connect to any external server, Firebase, or Supabase database.

Your business numbers, stock quantities, and product images remain 100% private and protected on your own mobile storage.
