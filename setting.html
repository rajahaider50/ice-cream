About Ice Cream & Slush POS

Ice Cream & Slush Stock Pro is a specialized offline stock management application designed specifically for ice cream shops, slush counters, and vendors managing cones, cups, spoons, syrups, and packaging.

Developer / Creator Details:

App Name: Ice Cream & Slush Stock Pro

Environment: HopWeb / Android WebView Ready PWA

Version: 2.5.0 (Offline Release)

Built with instant touch response and native Android interface feel.

Terms & Conditions

1. All inventory quantities, product sizes, cost prices, selling prices, and history logs are saved exclusively within your mobile browser or Android app storage (LocalStorage).

2. Clearing browser cache without taking a JSON backup will erase local records. We strongly recommend exporting backups regularly via the Export Backup tool.

3. This application operates standalone without requiring user credentials, login, or internet servers.

Security & Privacy Policy

100% Zero-Server Privacy: No inventory data, photos, or numbers leave your smartphone. There is zero integration with external databases (No Firebase, No Supabase).

Your shop data is completely private, secure, and under your direct physical device control at all times.
